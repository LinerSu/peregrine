Dear Hiring Team,

I'm writing to apply for the Backend Engineer role at Northwind Robotics. Your
posting's emphasis on shipping reliable services for autonomous systems maps
directly to the last three years of my work, where I owned the order-routing
service that handled millions of requests a day at 99.98% availability.

Two things in your description stood out. First, you want someone who can move
between product and platform work — at my current team I built both the
customer-facing checkout flow and the internal feature store that backed it, so
I'm comfortable owning a feature end to end. Second, you call out a strong
testing culture; I introduced contract tests that cut our staging incidents by
roughly 40%, and I'd bring the same discipline here.

I'm drawn to Northwind because the problem — making robotics dependable in the
real world — is one where reliability isn't a nice-to-have but the product. I'd
welcome the chance to talk about how I can help your team ship faster without
trading away that reliability.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
Jordan Avery
