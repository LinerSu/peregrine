Dear Dr. Okafor and the Genomics team,

I am applying for the Computational Biologist position at Verdant Biosciences.
My doctoral work centered on single-cell methods for rare cell populations, and
your team's focus on target discovery from primary tissue is exactly the kind of
problem I want to spend the next stage of my career on.

In my thesis I built a reproducible pipeline that processed a 200,000-cell atlas
and surfaced a signaling program later validated at the bench — an experience
that taught me to design analyses with the wet-lab collaboration in mind from
day one, not as an afterthought. I'm comfortable owning the full path from raw
reads to a result a biologist can act on, and I care about pipelines that other
people can run without me in the room.

What excites me about Verdant is the chance to work where computational results
feed directly into experiments. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my
background in single-cell analysis and reproducible tooling could contribute to
your discovery efforts.

With appreciation,
Dr. Riley Chen
