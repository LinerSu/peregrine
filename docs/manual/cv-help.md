# Get CV help

**Goal:** build your profile from your CV, see what to improve, and tailor your CV to a job.

Everything here lives in the **Profile / CV** and **Upskilling** tabs.

## 1. Set up your profile

Your profile is what every fit score and draft is based on. Two ways to create it:

- **Parse your CV.** Paste your CV text into the assistant (*"parse my cv"* + the text), or
  use the **Profile / CV** tab to upload/point at a résumé. Peregrine extracts your name,
  headline, skills, links, and experience into a structured profile.
- **Drop a file in `resume/`.** Put a PDF / `.tex` / `.md` / `.txt` résumé in the `resume/`
  folder, then use **Import from resume/** on the Profile tab. (See *Edit your data directly*.)

The **Profile / CV** tab then shows a résumé-style page — education, experience, projects,
etc. — folded by section, with your links rendered as icons.

## 2. See what to improve

Open the **Upskilling** tab (or ask *"what skills am I missing?"*). Against a job you've
evaluated, Peregrine lists the **skill gaps** and concrete suggestions to close them, so you
know what to learn or highlight.

## 3. Tailor your CV to a job

When you're preparing to apply (see *Prepare & apply*), Peregrine can generate a **tailored
CV** that reframes your real experience toward a specific posting — it never invents
experience you don't have. You can export it (including a LaTeX/PDF version) from the job's
**Prepare to apply** flow.

## Tips

- Re-parse your CV any time you update it; the profile refreshes.
- The richer your profile, the better the fit scores and drafts.
- For real extraction (not mock), use an AI key or **Internal (Claude)** mode.
