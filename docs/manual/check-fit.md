# Check your fit

**Goal:** find out how well you match a job, and why.

## Evaluate a job

1. Open the **Jobs** tab and click a job to open it.
2. Click **Evaluate fit**.

Peregrine reads the posting, compares it to your profile, and writes a **scorecard**:

- a **fit score**,
- **strengths** — where you match,
- **weaknesses / gaps** — what's missing or weak,
- **materials to prepare** — what you'll want before applying,
- a **recommendation** (apply / hold / skip) and a legitimacy read on the posting.

The score also appears in the **Fit** column back in the jobs list, and the job moves under
the **Evaluated** tab.

> Want a real, reasoned evaluation rather than a placeholder? Make sure you're not in mock
> mode — set an AI provider key, or use **Internal (Claude)**. See *External vs Internal mode*.

## Ask in chat

You can also drive it from the assistant panel:

- *"evaluate my fit for the Anthropic role"* — runs the same evaluation.
- *"what am I missing for &lt;company&gt;?"* — focuses on the gaps.

## Compare several

Evaluate a few jobs, then sort the jobs list by the **Fit** column to see your strongest
matches at the top. The **Insights** tab summarizes patterns across what you've evaluated and
applied to.
