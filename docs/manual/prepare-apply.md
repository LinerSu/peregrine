# Prepare & apply

**Goal:** turn a good-fit job into a ready application — and decide to apply with a clear view
of where you stand.

## 1. Prepare your materials

Open the job (Jobs tab → click the row) and click **Prepare to apply**. Peregrine drafts:

- a **tailored CV** for this posting (your real experience, reframed — nothing invented), and
- a **cover letter** that draws on your background and any past letters you've shared as
  samples.

You can review and regenerate these from the job view.

## 2. The review gate

The **Apply** button is intentionally gated. Before it unlocks, Peregrine shows you a
**review**: your strengths for this role, your gaps, and the materials it prepared. This is
the moment to decide honestly whether to apply, fix a gap first, or skip.

Peregrine never submits an application for you. **Apply** is your action — it opens the real
posting so you apply on the company's own site.

## 3. Mark it applied

Once you've applied, click **Mark applied** (or **Apply**, which records it). The job is
logged as an application with today's date and moves into your **Applications** tracker.

## Applied somewhere else already?

If you applied through another channel, you don't have to redo the flow — see
*Track applications* for **+ Add** and **+ From posting** (paste/PDF/URL).
